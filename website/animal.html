Animal Vision Simulator prototype

Live camera or photo, rendered as a dog, cat, fly or bee would see it: species photoreceptor spectra, acuity and compound-eye sampling, all computed in scene degrees.

Space bar toggles the split view. Left of the line is the original, right is the animal.

    What this can and cannot show. Colour is simulated the Vischeck way: each pixel is treated as a colour on a measured display, the animal's receptor catches are computed from published spectral peaks, and the result is re-rendered so that the animal could not tell the simulation from the original. Ultraviolet channels (flies, bees, birds) cannot be recovered from an RGB camera and are left out. Acuity blur is applied in scene degrees, so on a phone it is nearly invisible unless you enable true scale, which is the physically correct behaviour. Nothing here says what the animal experiences.
